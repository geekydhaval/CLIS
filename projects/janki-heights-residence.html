Janki Heights Residence

Janki Heights, Surat
Residential · Apartment

Location
Janki Heights, Surat
Type
Residential: two-bedroom apartment
Scope
Interior design and working drawings
Status
Design stage, September 2026
Spaces
Living room, kitchen, two bedrooms

A two-bedroom apartment at Janki Heights with a mural for a headboard wall.

The master bedroom is the centre of this home. A chinoiserie mural of cranes and flowering branches runs the full width behind the bed, with a soft curve at its corner and light washing down from above. A second option keeps the same bed against bookmatched stone.

The living room stays simple so the bedroom can speak: a fluted dado behind an L-shaped sofa, a floating TV console, and warm cove light along the ceiling.
