Aspire, Tower K

Aspire, Surat
Residential · Apartment

Location
Aspire, Surat
Type
Residential: three-bedroom apartment
Scope
Interior design and working drawings
Status
Design stage, July to September 2026
Spaces
Entrance, living, dining, kitchen, three bedrooms

A family apartment at Aspire built around light that comes from behind things.

The living room pairs a backlit stone panel with a fluted TV wall. Opposite it, an arched niche glows from its edge and a slotted screen lets light through to the dining side. The dining wall is a field of folded panels under a crystal chandelier.

The kitchen repeats the arch as a doorway, with a green stone backsplash and tall units in dark fluted glass. The bedrooms are deliberately plain: beige laminate, slim grooves, an oval mirror, one pendant by the bed.
