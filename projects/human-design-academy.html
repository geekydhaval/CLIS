Human Design Academy

Adajan, Surat
Commercial · Learning studio

Location
Adajan, Surat
Type
Commercial: learning studio and workplace
Scope
Space planning, interior design, ceiling, lighting and furniture drawings
Status
Design stage, October 2026
Spaces
Reception lounge, meeting booths, conference room, cabin, stage, game zone, cafeteria

A learning studio in Adajan, drawn to feel nothing like an office.

You arrive at a circular lounge under a ring of light, with a moss wall behind it and the academy's monogram glowing on peach plaster. From there the floor opens into one long room: booths wrapped in planting for small conversations, a glass conference room, a cabin with a daybed, and a stage where the seating is a scatter of pastel pebbles.

The ceiling does as much work as the walls. A field of triangular panels runs over the common area and breaks into curves above the lounge and the cafeteria, so each zone has its own light without a single partition.
