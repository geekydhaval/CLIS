Siesta Villa

Surat

Scope
Turnkey interiors
Status

Spaces

Photography

A villa named for the Spanish art of the afternoon pause.

Siesta Villa was designed to press pause on chaos. Warm white walls, pale marble and concealed light make a quiet base; against it sit a low blue-grey sofa, mustard cushions and a sculpted black screen by the stair.

The feature walls carry the character: arched panels in two blues behind the lounge, fluted oak set on the diagonal around the television, and a fluted-glass partition with planters that divides the dining room without closing it.
