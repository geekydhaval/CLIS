Sattvam Residence

Pal, Surat
Residential · Apartment

Location
Sattvam, Pal, Surat
Type
Residential: three-bedroom apartment
Scope
Interior design and working drawings
Status
Design stage, June 2026
Spaces
Foyer, living and dining, kitchen, three bedrooms

A three-bedroom home in Pal where every room gets one quiet gesture.

The foyer sets the tone with a wall of gingko leaves, each one backlit, so the first thing you see on coming home is a soft glow. The living room keeps to warm oak and stone around the TV wall, and a dining table folds out of a mural panel when it is needed.

In the bedrooms the same arch appears in three ways: routed into the wardrobe shutters, stacked as fluted panels behind a bed, and drawn in a single line across the master bedroom wall. Terracotta headboards carry the one strong colour.
